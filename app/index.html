PathLinks — for healthcare professionals

PathLinks is a clinical decision-support aid for registered UK healthcare professionals. It is not a registered medical device, is not a substitute for clinical judgement, and is not intended for use by patients or members of the public.

Guidance is aligned to NICE CKS/NG and BNF but may not reflect the latest updates. Always verify against the current NICE CKS, BNF/BNFc and your local formulary and policy before acting. In an emergency, follow local escalation pathways or call 999.

No patient-identifiable data should be entered. By continuing you confirm you are a healthcare professional and accept these terms.

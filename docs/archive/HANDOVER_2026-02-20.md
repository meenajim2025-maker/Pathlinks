## 2. Key Mental Health Upgrades (Consultant Grade)

### **Holistic Framework: Bio-Psycho-Social Overviews**
*   **New Section**: Every mental health module now starts with a **"Management Overview"** that frames the condition using the Bio-Psycho-Social model. 
*   **Consistency**: Ensures clinicians understand the philosophical approach (e.g., Validation in EUPD, Recovery in Addiction) before pharmacological titration.

### **Mood & Psychotic Disorders**
*   **Depression (TRD)**: Detailed **Treatment-Resistant Depression** protocols, including switching SNRI/NASSA and augmentation with **Lithium** or **Atypical Antipsychotics**.
*   **Bipolar Disorder**: Stratified management for Acute Mania vs. Bipolar Depression; includes Maintenance (Lithium) and toxicity monitoring.
*   **Schizophrenia (TRS)**: Integrated **Clozapine** as the mandatory 3rd-line for Treatment-Resistant Schizophrenia; emphasized physical health monitoring for metabolic syndrome.

### **Anxiety & Specialized Disorders**
*   **Phobias**: Implemented the **Graded Exposure** model for Specific/Social phobias and **Applied Tension** for Blood-Injection-Injury phobias.
*   **PTSD & OCD**: Integrated **EMDR**, **TF-CBT**, and **ERP** (Exposure & Response Prevention). Added Complex PTSD (C-PTSD) stabilization.
*   **ADHD**: Detailed stimulant classes (**Lisdexamfetamine** vs. **Methylphenidate** brands) and Shared Care Protocol (SCP) obligations.

### **Addiction & Behavioral Disorders**
*   **Alcohol (AUD)**: Integrated **CIWA-Ar** severity thresholds and the critical **Pabrinex-before-Glucose** mandate.
*   **Substance Misuse**: Added Opioid Agonist Treatment (OAT) and Harm Reduction strategies.
*   **Eating Disorders**: Implemented the **MARSIPAN** emergency risk triad (HR <40, BP <90, Temp <35.5) for medical admission.
*   **Personality Disorders (EUPD)**: Built around **DBT (Dialectical Behaviour Therapy)** modules: Mindfulness, Distress Tolerance, Emotion Regulation, and Interpersonal Effectiveness.

### **Specialist & Legal Psychiatry**
*   **Perinatal MH**: High-priority safety planning for pregnancy/breastfeeding and **Postpartum Psychosis** emergency protocols.
*   **Dementia**: Stratified subtypes (Alzheimer's, Vascular, DLB, FTD) and **BPSD** management with strict antipsychotic warnings.
*   **Mental Capacity (MCA 2005)**: Detailed the **5 Core Principles** and the **2-Stage Functional Test** for clinico-legal assessments.
*   **Learning Disability (LD)**: Integrated the **STOMP** initiative and warnings against "Diagnostic Overshadowing."
*   **Bereavement**: Distinguished normal grief from **Prolonged Grief Disorder (ICD-11)**.
*   **MUS / Somatisation**: Implemented the nervous-system-dysregulation model for **FND** and chronic symptom management.

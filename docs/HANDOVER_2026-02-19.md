# Project Handover: PathLinks Clinical Refinement
**Date:** 2026-02-19
**Status:** Major Clinical Refinement Phase Complete

## 1. Executive Summary
The PathLinks project has undergone a significant transformation from a generic clinical decision support tool to a **Consultant-Level specialist guidance platform**. Every major clinical system has been systematically upgraded to align with current **UK Royal College** (RCP, BCS, BTS, BASH, BSG, BSR, BAUS, RCPCH, RCOphth) and **NICE** guidelines.

## 2. Key Clinical Upgrades (Consultant Grade)

### **Cardiovascular (BCS / RCP)**
*   **AF**: Integrated CHA2DS2-VASc, ORBIT bleeding risk, and Catheter Ablation triggers.
*   **Stable Angina**: Prioritized CTCA and evidence-based drug titration (RCP/BCS).

### **Neurology (RCP / BASH / ABN)**
*   **Headache**: Stratified care (Triptan+NSAID+Anti-emetic) and CGRP/Botox protocols.
*   **TIA/Stroke**: 24-hour clinic targets, 300mg Aspirin stat, and strict lipid targets (<1.8 LDL).
*   **Parkinson’s & MS**: Specialized diagnostic triads, MRI protocols, and relapse management.
*   **Vertigo**: Integrated Dix-Hallpike/Epley and HINTS exam for central vertigo.

### **Respiratory (BTS / SIGN)**
*   **Asthma**: BTS step-wise approach emphasizing MART therapy.
*   **Pneumonia**: CURB-65 stratified antibiotic policy (Amoxicillin vs Dual therapy).
*   **VTE**: Specialized Wells Score logic and PESI score for outpatient PE management.
*   **Chronic**: Added Bronchiectasis (ACBT), OSA (Epworth), and ILD (Fibrosis) pathways.

### **Rheumatology & Orthopaedics (BSR / BOA)**
*   **Inflammatory Arthritis**: 'Suspected Persistent Synovitis' urgent 3-week referral path.
*   **Back Pain**: Stratification into Mechanical vs Sciatica vs Stenosis (BOA/NICE).
*   **Adhesive Capsulitis**: Multi-stage management (Steroid injection vs Hydrodilatation vs MUA).

### **Women’s & Reproductive Health (RCOG / BMS / FSRH)**
*   **Menopause**: Transdermal preference, Micronised Progesterone, and Testosterone trials.
*   **Contraception**: UKMEC 2024 standards, LARC (8-year Mirena license), and missed pill logic.
*   **Gynaecology**: Endometriosis specialist referral and PCOS endometrial protection.

### **Paediatrics (RCPCH)**
*   **Croup & Bronchiolitis**: RCPCH standardized Dexamethasone (0.15mg/kg) and SpO2 admission criteria.
*   **Fever**: Traffic light system refined with specific 'Amber/Red' consultant triggers.

### **Gastroenterology & Urology (BSG / BAUS)**
*   **IBD**: Truelove & Witts criteria and Infliximab/rescue therapy thresholds.
*   **BPH/LUTS**: BAUS medical ladder and surgical triggers (TURP/UroLift).
*   **Scrotal Pain**: Emergency 6-hour torsion window and STI-stratified care.

### **Procedures & GP Operations**
*   **Minor Surgery**: lipoma/cyst criteria and absolute red flag exclusions.
*   **Joint Injections**: Detailed subacromial/knee/carpaltunnel steroid + lidocaine protocols.
*   **Palliative Care**: The "Big 4" SC anticipatory medications and Verification of Death.
*   **GP Ops**: Med3 certification rules, MASH safeguarding thresholds, and Polypharmacy (SMR).

## 3. Technical Implementation
*   **Core Data**: `scripts/management-content.json` handles all clinical logic.
*   **Build System**: `scripts/build-decision-tools.js` compiles the JSON into the protocol format used by the UI.
*   **UI/UX**: 
    - **Two-Column Layout**: Left-side controls, Right-side results.
    - **Sticky Guidance**: The management plan remains visible during scrolling.
    - **Aesthetics**: Glassmorphism, premium typography (Outfit/Inter), and micro-animations.
*   **Deployment**: Automated via Vercel (`npx vercel --prod`).
